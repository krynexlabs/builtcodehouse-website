Privacy Policy & Data Security

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Location: Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

BuiltCodeHouse ("we," "our," or "the Studio") operates as a digital engineering studio based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We are committed to protecting confidential engineering briefs, personal data, and proprietary systems requirements in compliance with international privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information Ingestion & Scope

When you submit an architecture brief via our technical consultation forms or communicate directly with our engineering principals, we collect: full name, corporate email address, organization identity, project type, objectives, timeline, and submitted technical specifications.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Information collected is strictly utilized to conduct technical feasibility analysis, coordinate engineering discovery discussions, and execute preliminary architecture scoping. We never sell, lease, or monetize client project information to any third party.

3. Cookies & Client-Side Storage

BuiltCodeHouse does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or profiling scripts. The website employs browser local storage solely for temporary inquiry redundancy backup (bch_inquiries) to prevent data loss in the event of network disruption. You may clear this cache at any time via your browser settings.

4. Mutual Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality

All project briefs received through BuiltCodeHouse channels are treated under default strict confidentiality. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) are executed prior to deep architectural data inspection or codebase access.

5. Studio Location, Data Rights & Governance

BuiltCodeHouse operates as a digital engineering studio based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. You retain the right to request full export, correction, or permanent deletion of your project briefs and contact metadata at any time by emailing contact@builtcodehouse.com. Formal corporate entity registration details and statutory filings are maintained under company records and will be provided upon bilateral contract execution.
